Creator Micro Studio Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

In short: Creator Micro Studio does not collect or transmit personal information. It configures a compatible Creator Micro directly over USB, and keeps its automatic-connect preference on your Mac.

Information the app handles

Creator Micro Studio does not include account sign-in, advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated cloud service. It does not send your key assignments, macros, encoder mappings, lighting settings, firmware values, or personal information to the publisher.

When connected, the app reads configuration from and writes configuration to the compatible Work Louder Creator Micro over its USB interface. The keypad stores its configuration. The app stores its automatic-connect preference in macOS app preferences on your Mac.

Network access

The app does not make network requests to transmit app data. If you choose the setup or support link in the app, macOS opens the Work Louder setup webpage in your browser. That website is operated by Work Louder and is governed by its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

The publisher does not receive app data and therefore does not retain it. The automatic-connect preference remains in macOS app preferences until you change or remove it. Keypad configuration remains on the keypad until you change or reset it with a compatible configurator or firmware procedure. Removing the app does not erase configuration stored on the keypad.

Children

Creator Micro Studio is a hardware configuration utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
